MAYURBHANJ MAP

Mayurbhanj is the largest district in Odisha by area. It spans 10,418 square kilometers across the northern boundary of the state. A map of Mayurbhanj shows a unique land-locked shape. It shares internal borders with the Kendujhar and Baleswar districts, and interstate boundaries with West Bengal and Jharkhand. Administratively, the map is organized into 4 subdivisions: Baripada (the district headquarters), Kaptipada, Bamanghati, and Panchapir. These are further mapped into 26 community development blocks, which feature major towns like Rairangpur, Karanjia, and Udala. Right at the heart of the Mayurbhanj geographical layout sits the massive Simlipal National Park and Biosphere Reserve. This area dominates the terrain with rolling hills, dense forest covers, and major river systems like the Budhabalanga and Kharkai that drain out radially across the district map.

TOURIST PLACE IN MAYURBHANJ

BHIM KUND

• Bhimkund is a deep blue natural reservoir formed by the Baitarani River plunging through a rocky gorge, named after the Pandava prince Bhima from the Mahabharata. • Mayurbhanj Side features well-maintained viewpoints, a tall watchtower, picnic spots, and the nearby Kundeswar Mahadev Temple.

SITA KUND

Sitakund Waterfall is a scenic destination located within Similipal National Park in Odisha's Mayurbhanj district. Named after Goddess Sita, who reportedly rested there during her exile, it features a deep rocky pool and a nearby Hanuman cave.

BALDIA DAM

Baldiha Dam is a historic medium irrigation project built across the Palpala River in the Shamakhunta block of Mayurbhanj, Odisha. Constructed in 1912 under Maharaja Sriram Chandra Bhanj Deo, its scenic stepped overflow structure and lush forest surroundings also make it a popular local picnic spot.

SULEIPART DHAM

The Suleipat Dam, also known as the Khadakhai Dam, is built across the Khadakhai River in the Mayurbhanj district of Odisha. It serves as a vital irrigation and water supply reservoir while doubling as a scenic local picnic spot.

LULUNG DAM

Lulung itself is a scenic eco-tourism destination and entrance gateway to the Similipal Biosphere Reserve on the banks of the Palpala River, the prominent reservoir located in its immediate vicinity is the picturesque Balidiha Dam. Situated roughly 17 to 25 kilometers from Baripada, this entire zone is highly popular for its mountain views, riverbed picnics, and lush forest landscapes.

DEULI DAM

Deuli Dam, also known as Jambhira Dam, is a scenic water reservoir built over the Jambhira River in the Mayurbhanj district of Odisha. It features a massive 7.5 km long embankment and serves as an important irrigation source as well as a popular picnic and nature spot.

SIMILIPAL

Similipal National Park, located in the Mayurbhanj district of Odisha, is a vast UNESCO Biosphere Reserve renowned for its dense sal forests and breathtaking waterfalls like Barehipani. It serves as a vital sanctuary for diverse wildlife, famously standing as the world's only natural habitat for rare melanistic (black) tigers.

RAM TIRTHO

Ramatirtha is a famous spiritual and ecological site located near Jashipur in the Mayurbhanj district of Odisha, situated right at the foothills of the Similipal National Park. It features an ancient temple dedicated to Lord Sri Rama alongside the district's only Mugger Crocodile Breeding Center.

LOCATION

Mayurbhanj is a culturally rich and landlocked district in northern Odisha, renowned for its vast tribal heritage, traditional Chhau dance, and the magnificent Similipal National Park. Historically the largest princely state in the region, it is celebrated for its lush forests, breathtaking waterfalls, and abundant mineral resources.
